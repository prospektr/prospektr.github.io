Privacy Policy – prospfind

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Summary

prospfind is an internal research tool. It has no public users, no login, and does not collect personal data from visitors or TikTok users.

2. What we process

Through the TikTok Commercial Content API we access publicly available information about advertisements, such as ad creative, advertiser name as published in the ad library, targeting regions, dates and reach figures provided by TikTok. We do not access TikTok user accounts, private data, contacts or messages.

3. What we do not do

• We do not identify, track or profile individual people.

• We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties.

• We do not store video files; only metadata and our own notes.

• We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

4. Purpose

The data is used only for internal analysis of advertising trends (formats, messaging, creative structure).

5. Retention

Ad metadata is deleted no later than 90 days after it was retrieved.

6. Security

Data is stored on a private, access-restricted system that is not reachable from the public internet.

7. Your rights

If you believe information relating to you has been processed, contact us and we will review and delete it where applicable.
